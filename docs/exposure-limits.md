# Exposure Limits

## Overview

Exposure limits prevent concentration risk and protect protocol solvency.

## Configurable Limits

- **Max Single Deposit**: Prevents outsized single positions
- **Daily Withdraw Limit**: Controls outflow velocity

## Default Values

- Max single deposit: 10 sBTC (1,000,000,000 micro-sBTC)
- Daily withdraw limit: 5 sBTC (500,000,000 micro-sBTC)
